About Daima Loans

Daima Loans is built on the Daima Foundation Theoryâ€”which states that digital financial empowerment is a fundamental right. By providing low-barrier micro-credit with flexible repayments, we cultivate long-term credit health and financial resilience for all Kenyans.

Why choose Daima?

1. High-Speed Processing: Unlike traditional banking that requires days of manual review, Daima operates on a 100% automated credit scoring engine. Pre-approved limits are credited directly to your Safaricom M-Pesa line in under 10 minutes, eliminating stressful queues.

2. Transparent Pricing: We charge a flat interest rate of 5% with absolute fee transparency. There are no compounding interests, hidden monthly account maintenance fees, or sudden rollover penalties. What you see on your dashboard is exactly what you pay back.

3. Credit Building Incentives: Timely repayments dynamically unlock higher loan limits up to Ksh. 50,000. Additionally, our automated systems fully refund the 5% delayed payment penalty back to your wallet upon timely settlement, protecting and boosting your CRB rating.

4. Secure Data Protection: Your personal information, ID card uploads, and selfie captures are safely compiled and stored using robust local encryption algorithms in strict accordance with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) guidelines.

5. Community-First Inclusion: Driven by the Jasper Markets development initiative, Daima Loans focus on empowering small-scale traders, farmers, and youth entrepreneurs across Kenya to meet urgent business cashflow or family emergency bills with accessible capital.

Legal & Regulatory Policies

Daima Loans operates under strict compliance with the legal frameworks governing digital credit provision in Kenya:

Central Bank of Kenya (CBK) Act Licensed and regulated as a Digital Credit Provider (DCP) in accordance with the CBK Amendment Act 2021. This ensures fair pricing models, prevents predatory lending, and guarantees total transaction transparency.
Data Protection Act (ODPC), 2019 All customer registration, identity documents (ID card uploads, selfie verification), and credit check data are processed under strict compliance with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) guidelines.
Consumer Protection Act, 2012 Guarantees consumers the right to complete cost-of-credit disclosures (interest, penalties, fees) before transaction signing. Protects borrowers from unsolicited billing and guarantees the right to fair repayments.
