Official Eligibility Requirements:

  • Kenyan Citizenship: Must be a Kenyan Citizen possessing a valid National ID Card.
  • Age Limit: Must provide birth details confirming applicant is aged between 18-65 years.
  • Mobile Line: Active registered Safaricom mobile ISP line (minimum 6 months activity).
  • Credit Standing: Active mobile money history with no active defaults on other mobile lending platforms.

Loan Repayment & Gateway Fees:

All Daima Loans carry a fixed 30-day maturity. Delay in repayment will attract an automatic penalty deduction of 5%, which will be fully refunded upon timely payments. An administrative manual M-Pesa Paybill facilitation fee is securely processed during submission to check line history and CRB score. Facilitation fees are fully refunded to your wallet in the event of an automated reject.
